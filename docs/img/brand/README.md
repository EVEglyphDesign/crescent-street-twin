## Palette — use exactly these

- Near-black: `#0a0a0a` — backgrounds and fields.
- White: `#ffffff` — primary type and the left crescent.
- Crescent orange: `#e87722` — primary accent, the right crescent, and **Twin** in the wordmark.
- Brighter orange `#ff8c2b` is reserved for interactive highlights only; it is not used in the supplied static assets.

No additional hues, color treatments, gradients, glass effects, shadows, or tinted surfaces. A black scrim is the sole permitted exception when required for legibility over live content.

## Type

Use Inter only. The wordmark uses Inter SemiBold with tight negative tracking; do not recreate it in another typeface, add italics, or loosen it. Body UI may use Inter Regular/Medium. Keep the wordmark’s mixed color treatment: white `The Crescent Street`, orange `Twin`.

## Minimum sizes and clear space

- **App mark:** 32px minimum in digital use. Use the provided `favicon-32.png` at that size; do not scale down an arbitrary larger export.
- **One-line wordmark:** 220px minimum width. Below that, switch to `wordmark_stacked.svg`.
- **Stacked wordmark:** 140px minimum width.
- Keep clear space on every side equal to at least the visible width of the `T` in `Twin` (or 8% of the lockup width, whichever is larger).
- Keep OG cards at their provided 1200 × 630 dimensions. Do not crop or overprint them.

## Do not alter the mark

- Do not separate, rotate, stretch, outline, crop, shadow, recolor, or place extra details inside the two crescents.
- Do not put the mark on a white, orange, photographic, glass, or gradient field; its near-black square is part of the mark.
- Do not add third shapes, strokes, letterforms, badges, or taglines inside the icon.
- Do not use the orange crescent alone as a substitute for the complete paired mark.

The paired crescents are intentionally simple: white left / orange right, facing across a central street-like negative space. Preserve that relationship at every size.
